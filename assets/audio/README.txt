POLICE RINGTONE ASSET
====================

Add your police siren/ringtone MP3 here as 'police_ring.mp3'.

Recommended:
- High-pitched police siren sound (~10-15s loop)
- Format: MP3, 128kbps, mono/stereo
- Free sources: Freesound.org, Zapsplat.com

The app will loop this ringtone until user answers or declines.
Safe for public alerting without embarrassment.
